Enter Agreement

These terms govern Fitto on this device. By checking the box and tapping Enter you agree to these Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy. The operator name is Fitto.

Fitto is a local training app. There is no Sign in with Apple, email, or password.

Device Local session

Enter creates a session stored on this iPhone. You can Log Out and return later; training history and F Coins remain unless you Clear all data or Delete Account.

You are responsible for the questions, comments, and media you add. Do not post another person’s private information.

Coins Paid content

F Coins unlock eligible form videos, premium courses, and course meal guides. Purchases go through Apple. Product IDs are com.fitto.app.lv1 through com.fitto.app.lv6.

  • Previewing a lesson is free.
  • Unlocking spends F Coins only after you confirm the spend sheet.
  • Duplicate App Store callbacks must not be charged twice.
  • Access stays with the local session on this device.
Fitto does not restore consumable F Coins from another device. Remaining coins are lost if you Delete Account here.

Q&A Community

Q&A, comments, and official coach chat must follow Community Guidelines. Do not post harassment, spam, sexual content, dangerous advice, or someone else’s private data.

Fitto may hide content, warn, or remove a local account that breaks these rules, the Privacy Policy, or App Store Review Guidelines. Use Report on a post or conversation, and Block to hide a name.

Form Fitness guidance

Lessons, meal guides, and coach replies are educational. They do not diagnose, treat, or replace medical care. Stop a set if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, numbness, or a sudden loss of strength.

Consult a qualified professional when an injury or health condition needs individual advice.

Chat Official coaches

Private chat is available only with official coaches. Other members cannot message you directly or open your personal profile as a social network.

Camera, Photos, and Microphone are requested only when you tap those buttons in chat or on your profile. You can cancel a capture and nothing is sent.

Leave Ending use

Log Out ends the session and keeps local data. Clear all data removes training and purchase records on this device. Delete Account wipes the local session, media, questions, entitlements, and remaining F Coins.
