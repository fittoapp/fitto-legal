Fitto Who operates Fitto

Fitto (“Fitto”, “we”) operates this app. The operator name is Fitto. This policy describes training, nutrition, and community features that run on your iPhone.

Fitto is a local training studio: workout logs, body metrics, questions, and F Coin entitlements are stored on this device. Fitto does not create an Apple ID, email, or password account.

Local Information we use

Depending on the features you use, Fitto may store:

  • Profile details you enter (display name, username, bio, and an optional photo)
  • Workout lists, actual sets, rest length, training frequency, and history
  • Height, weight, and BMI calculated on this device
  • Questions, comments, saved questions, and official coach chat messages
  • Photos, videos, or voice notes you attach after you tap Camera, Photos, or Voice
  • F Coin balance, welcome grant, confirmed spends, and App Store product receipts kept in a local ledger
  • Reports, blocked names, and community-safety choices

Fitto does not collect IDFA and does not use App Tracking Transparency.

Use How it is used

Fitto uses this information to save your training, show BMI and weight history, unlock paid videos, courses, and meal guides after a confirmed spend, and keep official coach conversations on this device.

Fitto does not sell personal information. Fitto does not share training or chat content with a third-party AI service.

Tap Permissions

Camera is requested only when you tap Take Photo or Camera, to capture a profile picture or a training photo or video for official coach chat.

Photos is requested only when you tap Choose from Photos or Photo Library.

Microphone is requested only when you tap Voice message in official coach chat.

Notifications may be requested when the app becomes active so Fitto can remind you about training. You can refuse any permission and keep using the rest of the app.

Refusing Camera, Photos, or Microphone never blocks Enter, logging sets, or browsing Q&A. Those tools stay unused until you tap them.

Coins Purchases

F Coins are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. Packs are identified as com.fitto.app.lv1 through com.fitto.app.lv6. Fitto records a local ledger of grants and spends. Duplicate App Store callbacks are applied once.

Unlocking a form video, premium course, or meal guide spends F Coins only after you confirm. Previewing a lesson is free. Remaining F Coins are lost if you Delete Account on this device.

Q&A Community content

Questions, comments, and official coach messages are user-generated content stored locally for the features you use. You can report content, block a name, and review Community Guidelines inside the app.

Direct messages are limited to official coaches. Other members cannot open a private chat with you.

You Your choices

Log Out ends the local session and returns to Enter. Training records, questions, and F Coins stay on this device.

Clear all data removes local training and purchase data while leaving you signed in until you also leave the session.

Delete Account permanently removes the local session and wipes records, media, questions, entitlements, and remaining F Coins on this device.

Help Contact

Complaints, privacy questions, and safety reports: open Settings → Help & Support inside Fitto. Operator name is Fitto.

Fitto is not intended for children who cannot understand these terms. Training and nutrition material is educational and does not replace medical care.
