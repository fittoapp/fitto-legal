Effective October 2026 Operator · Fitto

Floor How we train together

Fitto Q&A, comments, and official coach chat are for training help. These rules apply to every post, comment, question, and message. Operator name is Fitto.

No Prohibited content

  • Harassment, threats, bullying, or targeted abuse
  • Sexual, violent, or otherwise unsuitable material
  • Spam, scams, or deceptive links
  • Advice that could cause harm, or sharing another person’s private information
  • Infringing someone else’s work

Flag How reports are handled

Use Report on a question, comment, profile, or conversation. Choose a reason. Fitto reviews reports and may remove violating content.

Hold Account penalties

Fitto may warn, hide content, suspend, or delete an account that breaks these guidelines, the Terms of Use, or App Store Review Guidelines.

Hide Block

You can block a user so their posts, comments, and profile stay hidden for you. Unblock from Settings → Blocked users.

Help Contact

Complaints and safety issues: Settings → Help & Support. Operator name: Fitto.
